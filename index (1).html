Ask an expert

Wellness

Good health is more than food. Small daily habits around sleep, movement and calm add up.

Sleep

Keep a regular bedtime, dim the lights an hour before bed and keep screens out of the bedroom. Most adults do best with 7 to 9 hours.

Calm

Try five slow breaths when stress rises, or a short stretch or yoga session. Even ten quiet minutes a day helps.

Movement

Aim for regular activity you enjoy, such as walking, cycling or swimming, and add strength work a couple of times a week.

Hydration

Sip water through the day. Log your glasses with the water tracker in Health tools.

How to build a balanced plate

Fill about half your plate with vegetables and fruit, a quarter with whole grains and a quarter with protein such as beans, fish, eggs or lean meat. Add a little healthy fat, like olive oil or nuts, and drink water with your meal.

Reading a nutrition label

Read article

Start with the serving size, since every number refers to it. Then check calories, fibre, sugar and salt. A short ingredient list with foods you recognise is usually a good sign.

Frequently asked questions

Can GreenPlate replace my doctor or dietitian?

No. GreenPlate shares general education only. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet, especially if you have a medical condition.

How accurate is the BMI calculator?

BMI is a quick screening number. It does not measure body fat or muscle, so use it as a starting point, not a diagnosis.

Where do the calorie estimates come from?

They use a standard equation based on your weight, height, age and activity. Real needs vary from person to person.

Is my information stored?

Your tracker ticks and messages are kept only in your own browser on this device.
